# Capstone Research Project

## Impact of Internal Migration and Social Support on Academic Outcomes Among Secondary School Students in Abuja, Nigeria

### Project Overview
This quantitative research project examined the relationships between internal migration, social support, sense of belonging, mental health indicators, and academic performance among secondary school students in Abuja, Nigeria.

### Data and Methods
- Quantitative secondary data analysis
- Study sample: 350 secondary school students
- Descriptive statistics
- Welch two-sample t-tests
- Pearson chi-square tests
- Correlation analysis
- Statistical analysis using R

### Key Findings
The analysis found differences between migrant and non-migrant students in academic performance and social support. Social support and sense of belonging were positively correlated with examination scores, while depression scores were negatively correlated with examination scores.

### Skills Demonstrated
- Quantitative research
- Data cleaning and analysis
- Statistical interpretation
- R programming
- Research communication

### Note
The findings describe associations and should not be interpreted as proof of cause and effect.
