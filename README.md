# Data Analytics for Management, Leadership & Business Decisions

**Healthcare Management | Business Performance | Data Analysis & Strategic Recommendations**

I use available data to identify patterns, evaluate performance, uncover opportunities for improvement, and develop practical recommendations that support better management, leadership, and business decisions.

With experience in healthcare operations, laboratory management, quality systems, and business performance, I am interested in turning data into actionable insights that help organisations improve efficiency, strengthen performance, manage resources effectively, and achieve their goals.

My approach goes beyond presenting numbers. I focus on understanding what the data means, why it matters, and what decision-makers can do with the findings.

## Areas of Interest

- Data-driven management and leadership
- Business performance analysis and growth opportunities
- Operational efficiency and resource optimisation
- Performance monitoring and evidence-based decision-making
- Data visualisation and dashboard development
- Statistical analysis using R and other analytical tools

## What I Aim to Deliver

I aim to help organisations move from raw data to useful decisions by:

1. Analysing available data to understand performance.
2. Identifying trends, gaps, risks, and opportunities.
3. Explaining the implications of the findings.
4. Recommending practical actions based on the evidence.
5. Supporting performance monitoring and continuous improvement.
